### Night Crickets

- **R2 object:** `audio/night-crickets.wav`
- **Local file:** `public/audio/night-crickets.wav`
- **Source:** Summer night crickets loop, Mixkit sound effect 1789
- **Author:** Not documented
- **License:** [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree), separate from Slowbit's MIT source-code license.
- **Source page:** https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/crickets/
- **Original recording:** https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1789/1789.wav
- **Downloaded:** 2026-10-08
- **Usage:** Night ambience in Slowbit's interactive mixer, loaded from the configured public asset URL. Do not redistribute as a standalone stock sound.
- **Processing:** Two gentle 6 kHz low-pass stages, reduced level (target RMS up to 0.009; peak capped at 0.05), and a two-second equal-power loop crossfade. Output: 40 seconds, stereo, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
- **Reproduce:** `python3 scripts/prepare-night-audio.py /path/to/1789.wav`
